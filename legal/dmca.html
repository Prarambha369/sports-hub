Our position

Kick Sports respects intellectual property rights and complies with the Digital Millennium Copyright Act (DMCA) and applicable copyright law. We publish original editorial content and publicly available score data. We do not host, store, stream or distribute copyrighted match footage.

Filing a takedown notice

If you believe content on this site infringes a copyright you own or represent, send a written notice to our designated agent including:

  • Your full legal name and contact details.
  • A description of the copyrighted work you say has been infringed.
  • The exact URL(s) on this site where the material appears.
  • A statement of good-faith belief that the use is not authorised by the owner, its agent, or the law.
  • A statement, under penalty of perjury, that your notice is accurate and that you are the owner or authorised to act for the owner.
  • Your physical or electronic signature.
Send notices to: dmca@example.com (replace with your agent's address). We aim to respond to valid notices within 48 hours.

Counter-notice

If your content was removed in error, you may submit a counter-notice with the information required under the DMCA. Valid counter-notices lead to reinstatement within 10–14 business days, unless the complaining party files a court action.
