Cookies Policy

Last updated: 1 January 2025

1. What cookies are

Cookies are small files stored on your device to help a site remember preferences and understand usage.

2. Types we use

  • Strictly necessary — required for the site to work (session, security). Always on.
  • Analytics — aggregated usage insights. Set only after consent.
  • Advertising — used by ad networks to show relevant ads. Set only after consent.

3. Consent (GDPR)

On your first visit we show a consent banner. You can Accept All or Reject Non-Essential. No analytics or advertising scripts load before you accept. Your choice is stored in your browser's localStorage.

4. Managing your choice

Reopen the banner any time via the Cookie settings link in the footer, or clear this site's localStorage and reload. You can also control cookies in your browser settings.

5. Third-party cookies

Analytics and advertising cookies may be set by their respective providers once you consent. Please review those providers' own policies.

6. Retention

Necessary cookies are session-based. Analytics and advertising cookies persist per the relevant provider's defined lifetime.
