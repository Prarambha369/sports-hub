Partnership & Sponsorship

Advertising · Sponsored content · Affiliate deals

We work with brands, sportsbooks (where legally permitted), and content partners who want to reach an engaged sports audience.

What we offer

  • Display advertising slots (leaderboard, in-content, sidebar)
  • Sponsored posts and clearly labelled branded content
  • Affiliate placements for licensed streaming, ticketing and merch partners
  • Newsletter and social mentions

Our standards

  • All advertising must comply with local laws in the reader's jurisdiction.
  • We only promote licensed services — we do not partner with unlicensed streaming operators.
  • Gambling/betting ads are shown only where legal and with responsible-gambling notices.
